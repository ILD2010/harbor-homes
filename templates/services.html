When the Problem Can't Wait

Some home-service problems require more immediate attention. Significant plumbing leaks, electrical concerns, loss of heating or cooling during extreme conditions, and other urgent situations can quickly affect the safety or usability of your home.

Harbor provides assistance for qualifying urgent service needs and can help determine the appropriate next step based on the situation.

Examples of potentially urgent problems

Major Water Leaks

Unexpected or uncontrolled water that could damage the home.

Electrical Concerns

Electrical problems involving unusual heat, odors, sparking, or loss of power to important systems.

Heating & Cooling Failure

Loss of essential heating or cooling when conditions may create a serious concern.
