Questions? We're Here to Help.

From scheduling and service requests to estimates and customer accounts, we want working with Harbor to feel straightforward. Browse answers to some of the questions homeowners ask us most often.

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our team and we'll help point you in the right direction.

Scheduling & Appointments

How do I request service?

You can request service through our online service-request form or contact Harbor directly. Tell us what you’re experiencing, provide your contact information, and select a preferred scheduling window. Our team will review your request and follow up with the appropriate next steps.

Do I need to know which service I need?

No. You don't need to diagnose the problem yourself before contacting Harbor. Describe what you're seeing, hearing, or experiencing, and our team can help determine whether plumbing, electrical, heating and cooling, or home maintenance is the best place to start.

Can I choose a preferred appointment time?

Yes. When submitting an online request, you can provide your preferred day and time window. Requested times are preferences rather than confirmed appointments. Harbor will contact you to confirm scheduling and availability.

What happens after I submit a service request?

Our team reviews the information you provided, determines the appropriate service category, and contacts you using your preferred contact method. Once the details and scheduling are confirmed, Harbor coordinates the appropriate technician or service team.

Services

What types of work does Harbor provide?

Harbor provides residential plumbing, electrical, heating and cooling, and general home-maintenance services. Individual services range from troubleshooting and repairs to installations, preventative maintenance, and system replacements.

Does Harbor handle small home repairs?

Yes. Our home-maintenance services are intended for many of the smaller repairs and preventative tasks that don't necessarily require a larger plumbing, electrical, or HVAC project.

Can Harbor help with more than one problem during a visit?

Let us know about each issue when requesting service. Depending on the type of work involved, scheduling, and the technicians required, our team can help determine whether multiple needs can be addressed together or should be scheduled separately.

What if the service I need isn't listed on the website?

Contact us anyway. Our website highlights common residential services, but it may not represent every situation our team can assist with. Describe the problem and Harbor can help determine whether it falls within our services.

Estimates & Pricing

Can I get an estimate before work begins?

Pricing depends on the type of service, the condition of the system, and the work required. When possible, Harbor will explain the recommended work and applicable pricing before proceeding. Some situations may require an in-person evaluation before an accurate estimate can be provided.

Why can't every service have a fixed price online?

Homes and service problems vary considerably. Two issues that appear similar can require different parts, labor, diagnostics, or repairs. Rather than presenting pricing that may not accurately represent your situation, Harbor evaluates the specific service need before determining the appropriate work.

Will additional work be discussed with me?

If a technician identifies additional concerns or recommends work beyond the original request, the situation and available next steps should be discussed with the homeowner before additional work proceeds.

Emergency & Urgent Service

What qualifies as an urgent service request?

Significant water leaks, certain electrical concerns, and loss of essential heating or cooling during extreme conditions are examples of situations that may require faster attention. Contact Harbor and provide as much information as possible so our team can determine the appropriate response.

Should I use Harbor's website during a life-threatening emergency?

No. The online service-request system is not a replacement for emergency responders. If you believe there is an immediate threat involving fire, gas exposure, serious electrical danger, injury, or another threat to life or safety, contact the appropriate emergency service.

Does selecting “Urgent” guarantee immediate service?

No. Selecting an urgent request helps Harbor understand the severity of the situation, but it does not guarantee immediate availability or a specific response time. Our team will evaluate the request and communicate available next steps.

Do I need an account to request service?

No. Homeowners can contact Harbor and submit a service request without creating an account.

Why should I create a Harbor account?

A Harbor account provides one place to manage your relationship with our service team. As portal features become available, customers can use their account to review service requests, access service history, manage account information, and keep track of work performed on their home.

Can I view previous service requests?

Registered customers can use the Harbor customer portal to access service information associated with their account as supported by the portal.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the password-reset option on the login page to begin recovering access to your account. For your security, Harbor staff should never need to ask you for your account password.

How will Harbor contact me about my request?

When submitting a service request, you can provide your contact information and preferred method of communication. Harbor will use the information associated with your request to follow up about service details and scheduling.

What information should I include in my request?

Describe what is happening, where in the home you're experiencing the problem, when you first noticed it, and anything else that may help our team understand the situation. You do not need to provide a technical diagnosis.

Should I submit sensitive information through the service form?

No. Only provide information relevant to your service request. Do not include passwords, financial account information, Social Security numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information in the problem-description field.

Still Have Questions?

Every home and every service request is a little different. If you couldn't find the answer you need, Harbor's team can help you determine the right next step.
