What Happens After You Submit?

Step 1 — We Review Your Request

Harbor reviews the information you provided and identifies the appropriate service team.

Step 2 — We Contact You

A member of the Harbor team follows up using your preferred contact method to clarify details and discuss scheduling.

Step 3 — Service Is Scheduled

Once the service and timing are confirmed, Harbor coordinates the appropriate technician or team.

Step 4 — Keep Track

Customers with a Harbor account can eventually review service requests and history through the customer portal.

Need Urgent Help?

Some home-service problems may require faster attention. Significant water leaks, electrical concerns, and loss of heating or cooling during extreme conditions may require urgent service.
