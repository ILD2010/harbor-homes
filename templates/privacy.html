Information You Provide

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when requesting service, contacting Harbor, creating an account, or using features of the customer portal.

This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Service address or ZIP code
  • Preferred contact method
  • Requested service category
  • Scheduling preferences
  • Information describing a home-service problem
  • Account login information
  • Service-request and service-history information

Customers should not submit passwords, financial account information, Social Security numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information through service-description or general contact fields.

How We Use Your Information

Harbor uses information collected through our website and services to provide and improve our home-service operations and customer experience.

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to service requests
  • Contact customers about requested services
  • Schedule and coordinate appointments
  • Determine the appropriate service team
  • Maintain customer accounts
  • Provide access to service history
  • Respond to questions or support requests
  • Maintain the security of our website and customer portal
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Improve our website and services
  • Meet applicable business or legal obligations

Service Requests

When you submit a service request, Harbor may collect the contact, property, scheduling, and service information necessary to review and respond to your request.

Information submitted through a service request may be made available to Harbor personnel who need it to coordinate, schedule, or perform the requested service.

Customer Accounts

Customers who create a Harbor account may provide information used to establish and maintain their account. Account information may be associated with service requests, service history, contact information, and other customer-portal features.

Customers are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials and should use a unique password that is not shared with other services.

Harbor employees will not ask customers to provide their account password.

Cookies and Website Usage

Like many websites, Harbor may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, maintain user sessions, remember certain preferences, and understand how the website is being used.

How Information May Be Shared

Harbor does not sell customer personal information.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to operate our services, fulfill customer requests, maintain our technology, comply with applicable legal requirements, or protect the security and integrity of our services.

How We Protect Information

Harbor takes reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

  • Encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Secure password hashing
  • Authentication controls
  • Authorization/access controls
  • Server-side input validation
  • Database access restrictions
  • Secure session management
  • Rate limiting
  • Logging and monitoring
  • Dependency/security updates

How Long We Keep Information

Harbor may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain customer and service records, operate customer accounts, meet legitimate business needs, resolve disputes, maintain security, and satisfy applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have certain rights or choices regarding your personal information.

Those could include requests to:

  • Access certain personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Update account information
  • Request deletion of certain information
  • Ask questions about how information is used
  • Manage certain communication preferences

Some information may need to be retained where required or permitted for legitimate business, security, recordkeeping, or legal purposes.

Children's Privacy

Harbor's website and customer services are intended for individuals seeking residential home services and are not designed for children. Harbor does not knowingly collect personal information from children through customer-account or service-request features where prohibited by applicable law.

Third-Party Services

Harbor's website may contain links to or integrate with third-party websites and services. Those services may maintain their own privacy practices, and Harbor's Privacy Policy does not govern information collected independently by third parties.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

Harbor may update this Privacy Policy as our website, services, technology, or legal obligations change. When updates are made, the revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
