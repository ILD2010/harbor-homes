Built on Service. Growing Through Trust.

Harbor Home Services is a family-owned home-service company providing plumbing, electrical, HVAC, and home-maintenance services to local homeowners since 2008.

It Started With One Truck

Daniel Mercer spent several years working in residential electrical and home repair. In 2008, he started Harbor with a single service truck.

His idea was that homeowners shouldn't have to maintain relationships with several different companies just to take care of one home.

Growing With the Communities We Serve

What began in 2008 as one service truck and a commitment to dependable workmanship gradually grew into something larger. As Harbor earned the trust of local homeowners, our customers began looking to us for help beyond the services we originally provided.

Rather than becoming a collection of disconnected contractors, we built a team around a simple idea: homeowners should have one company they can rely on for many of the systems that keep their homes running. Over the years, Harbor expanded its capabilities across electrical, plumbing, heating and cooling, and general home maintenance.

Today, Harbor Home Services has grown into a multi-trade team of 34 people while remaining privately owned and operated by the Mercer family. Our company may look different than it did with that first service truck, but the goal behind it remains the same—provide dependable service, communicate clearly, and build relationships that last beyond a single repair.

Harbor Today

Eighteen years after our first service call, Harbor has grown into a team of technicians, field supervisors, customer-service professionals, operations staff, and family leadership working toward the same goal: making home service easier to navigate.

Our 34-person team supports homeowners across four core service categories while maintaining the personal approach that shaped Harbor from the beginning. The Values Behind Our Work

Intro

Homes change. Technology changes. Companies grow. The principles behind good service shouldn't. These values guide how Harbor approaches our customers, our work, and the communities we serve.

Reliability

When a customer asks Harbor for help, we understand that they're trusting us with something important—their home. We believe that trust should be met with dependable communication, responsible service, and work our customers can feel confident relying on.

Clear Communication

Home repairs can already be stressful without leaving homeowners wondering what is happening. We believe customers deserve clear explanations of the problem, the work being recommended, and what they can expect next.

Respect for Your Home

We're guests in the homes we service. Our team is expected to approach every property with care, professionalism, and respect for the people who live there.

Relationships Over Transactions

Harbor wasn't built around completing one repair and disappearing. We want to become a company homeowners recognize, trust, and feel comfortable returning to as their homes and families change.

Let Our Family Help Take Care of Your Home.

For more than 18 years, Harbor has grown by helping homeowners take care of the places that matter most to them. Whether you're planning routine maintenance, looking for a trusted professional, or dealing with an unexpected repair, our team is ready to help you determine the right next step.
