One Team For The Home You Depend On

Taking care of a home often means relying on several different professionals. Harbor Home Services was founded in 2008 with a simpler idea: give homeowners one dependable company they can turn to for the services their homes need.

Today, our team brings together professionals across plumbing, electrical, HVAC, and general home maintenance. Whether you're dealing with an unexpected problem or planning ahead with routine maintenance, our goal is to make getting reliable home service straightforward from the first request to the completed job.

Questions Before Scheduling?

We want you to understand what to expect before requesting service. Here are answers to a few common questions.

Do You Provide Estimates?

Estimates depend on the type of service requested and the information available before an on-site evaluation. Harbor will communicate the applicable process before work is approved.

What services does Harbor provide?

Harbor provides residential plumbing, electrical, HVAC, general home-maintenance, and qualifying urgent-service assistance.

Do I need an account to request service?

No. Customers can contact Harbor and submit an initial service request without creating an account. An account provides additional tools for managing service history and future requests.
